# wiki/ — Curated knowledge (maintained by the assistant)

This is the **assistant's domain**: synthesized, linked, and kept up to date. Unlike `raw/`,
pages here are expected to be edited, merged, and rewritten over time.

**Contents**

| Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `index.md` | Master catalog of every wiki page — update on every create/rename/delete |
| `log.md` | Append-only activity log of what changed and why |
| `concepts/` | Concept and topic pages (one idea per page) |
| `projects/` | Project-specific pages (status, decisions, links) |
| `people/` | People dossiers (context, conversations, commitments) |

**Rules**

- Every page states its sources, linking back to the file in `raw/` or the URL it came from.
- Every page is linked from `index.md`.
- Every create, merge, rename, or delete is recorded in `log.md`.
- Prefer updating an existing page over creating a near-duplicate; note aliases instead.
- Distinguish clearly between what a source says and what is your own inference.
