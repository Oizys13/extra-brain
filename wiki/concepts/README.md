# wiki/concepts/ — Concept and topic pages

One page per idea, topic, framework, term, or recurring question. These are living
explanations that consolidate knowledge from many sources.

**What belongs here**

- Ideas you keep returning to (e.g. `spaced-repetition.md`, `retrieval-augmented-generation.md`).
- Definitions and mental models, with your own working understanding.
- Comparisons and synthesis across multiple sources.

**Conventions**

- Filename: lowercase, hyphenated, singular where natural (`concept-name.md`).
- Start with a one-line definition, then the details.
- Section for **Sources** linking to `raw/` files or URLs.
- Section for **Related** linking to other wiki pages.
- Clearly mark open questions and disagreements between sources.
- Add each new page to `wiki/index.md` and log it in `wiki/log.md`.
