# wiki/people/ — People dossiers

One page per person who matters to your work or life: collaborators, clients, authors you
follow, people mentioned across sources.

**What belongs here**

- Who they are and how you know them.
- Context, preferences, and communication style you've actually observed.
- Conversations, meetings, and commitments (with dates).
- Links to projects, journal entries, and sources that mention them.

**Conventions**

- Filename: `firstname-lastname.md` (lowercase, hyphenated).
- Note the **source** for every claim — memory, a conversation, a document.
- Keep sensitive details factual and relevant; this is a personal vault, not a place to
  speculate about people.
- Add the page to `wiki/index.md` and record changes in `wiki/log.md`.
