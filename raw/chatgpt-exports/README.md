# raw/chatgpt-exports/ — ChatGPT conversation exports

Raw exports of your ChatGPT account data. These are the ground truth for any conversation
you want to mine for ideas, decisions, or prompts.

**What belongs here**

- The official ChatGPT data export archive (`.zip`), extracted into its own dated folder,
  e.g. `2026-10-28/`.
- `conversations.json` — the full conversation history file.
- Message/attachment files included in the export (`chat.html`, images, etc.).

**Where to export it from**

1. Go to <https://chatgpt.com> and sign in.
2. Click your profile avatar → **Settings**.
3. Open **Data controls** → **Export data** → **Export**.
4. Confirm via the email link; the download link arrives by email (usually within minutes,
   sometimes up to 24 hours).
5. Download the `.zip` and extract it into a dated subfolder here.

Keep each export in its own dated folder so later exports don't overwrite earlier ones.
